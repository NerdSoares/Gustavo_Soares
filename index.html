Current project

Mapping Green Hydrogen Infrastructure Planning onto NISQ Devices: A QAOA Study of Electrolyser Siting

Poster · School on Quantum Simulation in the NISQ era · ICTP-SAIFR / IFT-UNESP, São Paulo · Nov 2026

Where should electrolyser plants go in Brazil’s Northeast? The siting problem is built entirely from open Brazilian data (ONS curtailment, OpenStreetMap substations, road distances, electricity prices), encoded as a 16-qubit QUBO and solved with QAOA in simulation and on IBM quantum hardware. The study maps which structural feature binds first: QAOA learns the constraints, but the cost differences between good plans are too small, inside the penalty-dominated energy scale, for the circuit to prefer the best one.

16 qubits4 candidate sites × 3 demand hubs
~140×valid plans found above chance (simulation)
21× / 20× / 9×on IBM Heron (ibm_kingston), 1–3 layers
